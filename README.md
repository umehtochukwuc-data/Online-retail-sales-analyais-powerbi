Online Retail Sales Analysis — Power BI
Project Overview
This project analyzes an online retail transaction dataset to evaluate sales performance, product contribution, customer value, geographical performance, and revenue trends.
The dataset initially contained 541,909 transaction records across 8 variables. Following data cleaning and preparation, 530,104 valid records were retained for analysis.
Business Objective
The objective of this project was to transform raw transactional data into an interactive Power BI dashboard that provides meaningful business insights and supports data-driven decision-making.
The analysis focused on:
Overall sales performance
Product performance
Customer value
Geographical revenue
Sales trends over time
Potential business opportunities and risks

Data Preparation
The raw dataset was cleaned and transformed using Power Query.
Key preparation steps included:
Correcting data types
Removing cancelled transactions
Removing records with non-positive quantities
Removing records with non-positive unit prices
Creating a Revenue field
Creating a date-only field for time-based analysis
After cleaning, 530,104 records were retained for analysis.

Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard consists of three analytical pages:
1. Executive Sales Overview
Provides a high-level view of revenue, orders, customers, quantity, average order value, monthly performance, products, and geographical revenue.
2. Product & Customer Analysis
Examines product performance by revenue and quantity, customer revenue contribution, and customer segmentation.
3. Sales Performance & Trends
Analyzes revenue trends over time, geographical performance, and the relationship between revenue and order activity.
Key Business Insights
Geographical Performance
The United Kingdom generated approximately £9.03 million, making it the dominant revenue-generating market.
Product Performance
Paper Craft, Little Bridge recorded the highest quantity sold at 80,995 units. Product rankings differed when measured by revenue and quantity, showing that sales volume does not necessarily correspond directly to revenue contribution.
Customer Performance
Revenue was concentrated among a relatively small group of high-value customers. The highest-revenue customer generated approximately £280,206.
Sales Trends
November 2011 recorded the highest monthly revenue at £1,509,496.33, while February 2011 recorded the lowest at £523,631.89.
Business Recommendations
Explore opportunities to expand sales beyond the UK.
Develop retention strategies for high-value customers.
Consider both revenue and quantity when making product and inventory decisions.
Investigate the factors behind particularly strong and weak sales periods.
Separate merchandise revenue from postage and administrative items when evaluating product performance.
